How to play

It starts as chess. Every capture rewrites the board.

♟Take a piece, take its power. Your capturing piece inherits the captured piece's movement for the rest of the game.
♚Kings are the exception. A king can capture, but never changes power. Kings cannot be captured.
↗Play to checkmate. Standard check, checkmate, pawn promotion, and castling rules apply. Captured powers are shown on the board.
⌖Local two-player: choose a piece, then a highlighted destination. Use Undo to take back a move.
